FAQ

Is there a bonus for larger top-ups?

50–999 € → +20% credits. 1000 € → +50% credits. Examples: 50→60 · 100→120 · 500→600 · 1000→1500. Amounts 1–49 have no bonus.

Can I use decimals (10.50 €)?

No. Only whole numbers from 1 to 1000.

Do credits expire?

No automatic expiry. Use them whenever you want on visibility services.

Can I pay for one day without topping up?

The model is credits-only. If you lack balance, top up and spend in My ad.

How do I pay with Bizum?

When you buy a pack you get a code EB-XXXX. Send the amount via Bizum with that reference and confirm in checkout.
